Who this policy is about

This policy covers the website at ironadamant.com, run by Aron Amos, trading as Iron Adamant, a sole trader in Western Sydney, NSW, Australia. ABN: 51 477 506 281. Contact: aron.amos@ironadamant.com.

What the contact form collects

The contact form on this site asks for:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • a subject
  • a message

Those fields are sent to Formspree (formspree.io), which delivers the submission to me by email. The form endpoint used by this site is Formspree form xjkrzwlq. The form does not ask for a phone number. Please do not put passwords or other secrets in the message.

If you email aron.amos@ironadamant.com directly, I receive the address you wrote from and whatever you chose to include.

Why I collect it

I use the form and direct email to read your enquiry, reply, and keep a record of what you asked for if we later agree a scope and a price. I do not use it for advertising mail-outs.

Formspree

Formspree is a separate service. It receives the form submission so it can email me. Formspree may store or process that submission on servers outside Australia. How Formspree handles the submission is described in Formspree's privacy policy.

Hosting and what this site does not run

The public pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. This site does not use a third-party advertising or analytics service. Your browser may store a service-worker cache so pages can load again. That cache stays on your device. I do not use it to build a profile of you.

I do not sell personal information.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiry emails for as long as I need them to reply, to remember what was asked, and to meet a legal record-keeping duty if one applies. You can ask me to delete an enquiry by email. I may keep a copy where I need it for a legal claim or a legal duty.

Access and correction

Email me if you want to know what I hold about you from this site, or if you want it corrected. If you are not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If I change this policy, I will update this page and the date at the top.

About Aron

I’m Aron Amos (Iron Adamant), based in Australia and working remotely. I build and ship practical systems for contract clients: custom apps, AI-assisted tooling, and the ownership work of making the whole thing hold together.

A lot of that work has sat next to non-technical stakeholders. Years of that taught me how to take complicated detail and simplify it until people can actually use it to decide. Multi-party commercial and ops messes sit behind the software work, so I plan in layers and chase bottlenecks instead of polishing slides. Alongside client work I ship open-source tooling and my own products. If you need something built or made clearer, get in touch.

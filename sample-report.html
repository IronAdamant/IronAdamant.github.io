Example report: Example App

This is the shape of the note I send after a free first look. One journey. The top 5 fixes. Plain English for each one.

Summary

Example App lets someone book a desk for a day. I walked through it as a new person: create an account, then try to book a desk for tomorrow. The account looked created, and the booking page opened. Two of the five items below would stop that person finishing. The other three make the same journey harder to trust.

Journey walked

  1. Open the home page

    The page loaded, and Create account was the first action on the screen.

  2. Create an account

    I entered a name, left the email blank, and chose a password. Example App said the account was created.

  3. Land on the booking page

    The page listed desks for tomorrow. The date field had no visible name.

  4. Try to book a desk

    I chose a desk and confirmed. The page went blank, with no message about whether the desk was booked.

Top 5 fixes

  1. High. Sign-up accepts a blank email

    What this means: the person thinks they have an account, then cannot sign in, because there is no email to send a confirmation to.

  2. High. The account route returns bookings with no one signed in

    What this means: a private list of desk bookings can be read without an account. The route should refuse that request.

  3. Medium. Password reset does not fit a phone

    What this means: the reset link opens a wide page. The person cannot reach the save button on the phone they use for email, so they cannot finish the reset.

  4. Medium. A failed booking leaves a blank page

    What this means: the person does not know whether the desk was booked. They may book it twice, or give up.

  5. Low. The date field has no visible name

    What this means: the box is on the page, but nothing next to it says it is the date. Someone using a screen reader hears an unlabelled box.

Next steps

On a real free first look, this is the whole note: what I walked, the top 5 fixes, and what each one means. I do not start paid work from this page.

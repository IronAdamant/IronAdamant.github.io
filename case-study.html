Case study: DOER

DOER is an Australian professional network where people connect, learn, earn and build. Iron Adamant carried out an outside check of the new-member experience: sign-up, the Market, the courses, and accessibility across those journeys.

The work was delivered as a clear, organised findings report. Every issue raised was real and worth acting on, and the DOER team implemented the fixes.

Aron contacted us offering an outside check of DOER. He signed up as a new member and worked through sign up, the Market, courses and accessibility, then sent us a clear, well organised report. His communication was clear from his first email, and every finding he raised was real and worth acting on. I'm looking forward to communicating with Aron more.

Scott Barker, Founder, DOER
